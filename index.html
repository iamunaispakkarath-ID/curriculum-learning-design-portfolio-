Looking beyond what is taught to how it is learned.

During my internship with Mantra4Change, I reviewed a Grade 3 Social Science textbook and its teacher handbook. I examined how content, pedagogy and classroom guidance worked together, and identified areas where the material could be strengthened.

The review considered instructional structure, pacing, activities and assessment, with the aim of making the learning experience clearer and more purposeful for teachers and children.
